THE STARPOWER ARCHIVES

StarPower Interviews

Since 2010, StarPower Collective has asked people about the music that shaped their lives. Here are some of those conversations, in their own words.

  • Kool Mo Dee Hip-hop pioneer and member of the Treacherous Three
  • Inspectah Deck MC and producer, member of the Wu-Tang Clan
  • Onaje Allan Gumbs Jazz pianist, composer and arranger
  • Don Blackman Keyboardist, singer and songwriter from Jamaica, Queens
  • Reggie Reg MC with the legendary Crash Crew
  • Dennis Coffey Detroit guitarist, Motown session player and creator of "Scorpio"
  • Josh Milan Singer, songwriter and producer from Fort Greene, Brooklyn
  • Boyd Jarvis Producer, keyboardist and songwriter

Every StarPower interview plays in rotation on Nu Soul Hwy Radio.
