Josh Milan Has StarPower

Singer, songwriter and producer from Fort Greene, Brooklyn

Josh Milan on Fort Greene block parties, gospel at home and why he hears music as the voice of God.

Every StarPower interview plays in rotation on Nu Soul Hwy Radio. Tune in over the next couple of weeks and you'll catch Josh Milan's full show, with the music from their playlist.

The Story

Singer, songwriter and producer Josh Milan was born and raised in Fort Greene, Brooklyn. On StarPower Collective, he remembered a neighborhood that was poor but close, and said that togetherness is what he misses most.

His parents were church people, so gospel filled the house, while right outside the door the block was scratching and rapping. Anyone with two turntables and a mixer could rock a block party, and music was simply a way of life. When he first heard "The Message," he took it home and played it all night on his mother's stereo.

His first concert was a church choir day, and he still feels that same rush when he sees a show today, as he did recently at a Gregory Porter concert. He calls James Taylor a genius songwriter, praises bassist Derrick Hodge and names Quincy Jones as a lasting influence on his production.

For Josh, music is spiritual at its core.
