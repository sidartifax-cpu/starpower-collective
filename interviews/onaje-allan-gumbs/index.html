Onaje Allan Gumbs Has StarPower

Jazz pianist, composer and arranger

Pianist and arranger Onaje Allan Gumbs on music as healing, cooking up sound like a chef and his breakthrough with Norman Connors.

Watch on YouTube

The Story

Pianist, composer and arranger Onaje Allan Gumbs saw music as something meant to heal and nourish. On StarPower Collective, he compared musicians to chefs: it starts with the heartbeat, then the melody, and his mission was to serve listeners every course in the world of sound.

He traced his first major break to drummer Norman Connors, who heard him and asked him to arrange music for a record. That assignment became the title track of Connors' album "Dark of Light," and the musicians on the session were jazz royalty. Connors was so impressed he encouraged Onaje to take the next step in his own career.

As a young listener, Onaje fell so hard for film scores that he wanted every record a composer made and to see every movie that composer scored. He also credited the late Dr. Billy Taylor, the pianist and educator he heard on the radio, as a big influence.
