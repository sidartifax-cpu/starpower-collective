Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

StarPower Collective ("we," "us") runs starpowercollective.com. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we collect only what we need, we don't sell your information, and we don't share your story without your consent.

Information you give us

Guest applications. When you apply to be a guest, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, city, biography, interview topics, links and availability. We use them only to review your application and contact you.

Immoortal Souls Legacy Call requests. We collect your name, email, optional phone number, who you'd like to honor, and whether you want to join the Founding Families list. We use this only to contact you about Immoortal Souls.

Email signup. If you sign up for updates, we collect your email address and optional first name to send you news about new interviews, guests and merchandise. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email or contacting us.

Story tools. The practice questions and "My Life in Five Songs" tools run in your browser. What you type there is not sent to us unless you choose to send it.

Services that help run the site

We use a few trusted services. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Formspree receives and delivers our form submissions to us.
  • Shopify (through our family apparel brand, Amerukhan Basics) handles shop orders, payment and shipping. We never see or store your card details.
  • radio.co streams Nu Soul Hwy Radio when you press play and provides "now playing" information.
  • YouTube shows embedded interview videos. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode where possible.
  • Google Fonts provides the site's typefaces.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (if enabled) counts visits in aggregate without cookies and without tracking you across other sites.

Cookies and storage

We don't use advertising cookies. The site may save small preferences in your own browser, such as your radio volume. Shopify may set cookies needed for your cart and checkout.

Your stories and recordings

Interviews are published only with the guest's participation. If you are a guest, or a family member of a guest, and would like something changed or removed, contact us and we will respond personally.

How long we keep information

We keep form submissions only as long as we need them to respond to you, run the show and keep in touch, and we delete them on request.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you've shared, or to stop emailing you, at any time.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
