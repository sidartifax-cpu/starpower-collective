WHERE IT STARTED · HARLEM · 2010

It started with a crew, one mic and a simple belief: everyday people are Stars.

StarPower Collective interviews the people who shape our lives about the music that shaped theirs: the neighbor, the teacher, the DJ, the grandmother who still knows every word. We've also welcomed legends like Kool Mo Dee, Kidd Creole, Don Blackman and Onaje Allan Gumbs.

Each interview becomes a guest's own accolade, something they share proudly with family and friends. Some of our Stars have since passed, and their interviews were their last recordings. We gave those recordings to their families. That is why we call StarPower Collective our life's work.

THE FOUNDING FIVE

Five Minds. One Frequency.
A Legacy Beyond the Airwaves.

Derek B., Dano B., Ron J., Sid W. and Keith H. built StarPower Collective together, and with it a creative family: Nu Soul Hwy, Immoortal Souls and Psyba Village. Five voices, one shared belief: everyday people are Stars, and their stories deserve to live on.

  • DB

    Power

    Derek B.

    Founding Member

    For Derek, music is "the score, the soundtrack to our life." He grew up with his brother Dano on 129th Street between Convent Avenue and St. Nicholas Terrace, "a place we call the Hill." On Saturday mornings his father ran the record player, and Derek's job was putting the records back in their sleeves, where he fell in love with album art and liner notes. Years later, at a low point in his life, Derek came up with the idea for StarPower, a way to create "a better, loving space." His brother and friends helped him build it.

  • DB

    SuperStar

    Dano B.

    Founding Member

    Derek's brother Dano caught the DJ bug at ten years old. It was 1972, and their father's house parties brought guests carrying stacks of 45s with their names written on them. Dano stayed up late and manned the turntable. At Norman Thomas High School (1977–80) he turned the lunchroom into a daily party and threw school dances that drew students from all over the city. That's when, he says, "I realized my own star power." At 19 he moved to Park Hill, Staten Island, where he "grew up twice." For Dano, StarPower is "a sanctuary, a haven."

  • RJ

    Dast-One

    Ron J.

    Founding Member

    "Music is my life. Bottom line. Straight, no chaser." Ron grew up on 152nd and 153rd Street, down the block from the Jackie Robinson pool and a few blocks from the Polo Grounds. His mother played everything, from Funkadelic's "Cosmic Slop" on down, and Ron learned early not to sort music into genres: it's either good music or it isn't. Queen taught him how layered harmonies work, and Thin Lizzy, a rock band led by a Black frontman, Phil Lynott, opened his eyes to what a band could look like.

  • SW

    Da Creator

    Sid W.

    Founding Member

    For Sid, music is "the shower of life." He grew up on 127th Street between Convent Avenue and St. Nicholas Terrace, where family didn't stop at your front door. His grandfather's alarm-clock radio made Kenny Rogers' "The Gambler" an anthem of his childhood, and Saturdays meant records and Soul Train. After Martin Luther King Jr. High School, he spent more than 20 years in design and education, leading the Visual Design program at Westwood College. He's also a published poet (Sidisms: The Rants of a Man Mad) and a songwriter with more than 55 registered songs. For StarPower, Da Creator builds the look and sound: logos, websites, video editing and interview audio.

  • KH

    KV

    Keith H.

    Founding Member

    For Keith, music is a time machine. Hear a song from back in the day, close your eyes, and you drift back to a happier place and time. He grew up on St. Nicholas Terrace, on Da Hill, back when every parent on the block looked out for every kid and everybody knew everybody. In the '70s he caught the big shows at Madison Square Garden, where a drum solo could bring the whole arena to its feet, and he never forgot Parliament-Funkadelic's Mothership landing. Gil Scott-Heron's "The Revolution Will Not Be Televised" is on his playlist. Da Hill 4 Life.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is StarPower Collective?

StarPower Collective is an internet radio show from Harlem, New York, started in 2010. It interviews everyday people, and some well-known artists, about the music that shaped their lives. Every guest is treated as a Star.

Which artists have been interviewed on StarPower Collective?

StarPower Collective has interviewed Kool Mo Dee, Inspectah Deck, Onaje Allan Gumbs, Don Blackman, Reggie Reg, Dennis Coffey, Josh Milan and Boyd Jarvis, along with hundreds of everyday community members since 2010. Read their stories.

How can I listen to StarPower Collective?

Press Listen Live on starpowercollective.com. The show streams on Nu Soul Hwy Radio, and past interviews are on the StarPower Collective YouTube channel.

Who founded StarPower Collective?

StarPower Collective was founded by five friends from Harlem: Derek B. (Power), Dano B. (SuperStar), Ron J. (Dast-One), Sid W. (Da Creator) and Keith H. (KV).

How do I become a guest on StarPower Collective?

You don't have to be famous. Go to the Be a Guest section of starpowercollective.com, practice with real show questions, and send the guest application.

What is Immoortal Souls?

Immoortal Souls grew out of StarPower Collective. It helps families preserve an elder's voice, stories, photographs and wisdom while they are here to share them. Request a free Legacy Call on starpowercollective.com.

How do I contact StarPower Collective?

Email starpower.collective@gmail.com or call (678) 466-0560.

Where can I buy StarPower Collective t-shirts?

Right here. Get the StarPower Collective Tee or the Every Life Has a Soundtrack Tee. The Music Is the Shower of Life Tee is coming soon. Orders are fulfilled by Amerukhan Basics, our family apparel brand.

What do listeners say about StarPower Collective?

Listeners have called it a space of "peace, love and positive energy" and "the ultimate human connection through music, lyrics and song." One longtime listener wrote, "Thanks for keepin' StarPower Collective alive." Read their words and share your own StarPower story.
