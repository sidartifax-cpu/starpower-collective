WHERE IT STARTED · 2010

It started with four friends, one mic and a simple belief: everyday people are Stars.

StarPower Collective interviews the people who shape our lives about the music that shaped theirs: the neighbor, the teacher, the DJ, the grandmother who still knows every word. We've also welcomed legends like Kool Mo Dee, Kidd Creole, Don Blackman and Onaje Allan Gumbs.

Each interview becomes a guest's own accolade, something they share proudly with family and friends. Some of our Stars have since passed, and their interviews were their last recordings. We gave those recordings to their families. That is why we call StarPower Collective our life's work.

Do you have StarPower? Sure, you do.

THE LEGACY STARTER KIT

10 Questions Worth Asking

Use these questions to begin a meaningful conversation with someone you love.

  1. What is your earliest childhood memory?
  2. What was your neighborhood like growing up?
  3. Which family traditions meant the most to you?
  4. What song takes you back to your youth?
  5. Who taught you your most important lesson?
  6. What challenge changed the course of your life?
  7. What family story should never be forgotten?
  8. What are you most proud of?
  9. What wisdom would you share with future generations?
  10. How would you like to be remembered?
